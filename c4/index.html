FAQ

Quick answers

Where does the data come from?

Public sources only: Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, Google Trends and marketplace listings. Every figure comes with its URL and capture date.

Is this a "winning products" list?

No. You bring the product. We tell you whether to test it, fix the offer first, or skip it, and why.

Do you guarantee sales or ROAS?

No. Nobody honestly can. We guarantee every cited figure is correct and was captured no more than 30 days before delivery, or that report is refunded.

What is a capped test plan?

A budget ceiling, a kill rule and a stop date, written before you spend. So a bad test ends small.

How do I cancel Decision Desk?

Reply "stop" to any email. No annual plan, no trial that converts, no card stored by us.

Who are you?

An independent e-commerce research project in its pilot phase. No customers yet, which is why there are no reviews or logos here.
